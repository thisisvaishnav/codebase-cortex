# Hackathon Rules

> Registering for The Agent Harness Hackathon means agreeing to all of these rules, along with the WeMakeDevs Code of Conduct.

## Participation

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | Open to anyone online from anywhere in the world. Free to enter. In-person option available in San Francisco on August 29 (limited space, separate Luma registration required). |
| 2 | Solo or team of up to 4 members. Each participant may only be part of one team. |

## Required Technology

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 3 | **Agent must run on TrueForge.** A judge must be able to see the harness doing real work — not a thin wrapper around a model call. |
| 4 | **Code review is mandatory.** Every substantive change goes through a GitHub PR reviewed by Qodo before merging. Direct pushes to `main` do not count as reviewed work. |

> **Note on Qodo:** One installation per team is enough. Teammates don't need their own Qodo accounts. Fix every valid High-severity finding, or dismiss it with a reason in the Qodo thread. Medium/Low findings are your engineering call.

## What You Build

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 5 | Open-ended — any domain: developer tool, internal assistant, research desk, incident responder, data pipeline, etc. |
| 6 | **Must be open source.** Judges must be able to read the code and run it. |
| 7 | Only connect tools, data, and accounts you own or have permission to use. Keep private/login-protected info out of your repo and demo. |
| 8 | **Must be built during the hackathon** (coding and design work between 8:00 AM London on August 24 and the deadline). Pre-planning, diagrams, and notes are allowed. |
| 9 | Frameworks, open-source libraries, public APIs, templates, third-party tools, and publicly available assets are all allowed. |

## Submission Requirements (Rule 10)

Every submission must include:

- [ ] A public source-code repository
- [ ] A clear README with setup steps
- [ ] A demo video (~3 minutes) showing the agent working
- [ ] A short write-up of what the agent does and how it uses TrueForge
- [ ] A **`## Qodo Code Review Evidence`** section in the README:
  - Link to at least one representative merged PR with meaningful hackathon code
  - 1–2 sentences on what Qodo found and what you changed or dismissed
  - PR history showing the completed review, your decisions, and a follow-up review
- [ ] A link to your blog post (if entering the blog prize)

> **Note:** The public PR link is the required evidence. Screenshots may add context but cannot replace it.

## Deadline

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 11 | Submissions close **August 30 at 8:00 PM London time**. The schedule page shows this in your timezone. |

## AI Usage

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 12 | AI coding assistants are allowed but must be disclosed. |
| 13 | Participants must understand the submitted code and be able to explain the agent, architecture, and technical decisions. |
| 14 | Projects entirely generated by AI without meaningful participant contribution, verification, or understanding may be rejected. |

## Judging & Tracks

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 15 | Three judged tracks: **Best Use of TrueForge**, **Best Code Quality**, **Best UI**. Every submission is considered for all three, but one team can only win one track. |
| 16 | Blog post prize goes to one writer. Swag goes to 10 participants who share publicly and tag WeMakeDevs + TrueFoundry. |
| 17 | Everyone who submits a valid project gets a **certificate of participation** by email after judging. |
| 18 | TrueFoundry offers job interviews to teams behind top projects — no application needed. |

## IP & Conduct

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 19 | IP developed during the hackathon belongs to the participant/team. Agree on ownership internally before submitting. |
| 20 | Treat all participants, organizers, sponsors, speakers, judges, and community members with respect. |
| 21 | Harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, or manipulating judging = **disqualification**. |
| 22 | Failure to follow rules or the WeMakeDevs Code of Conduct may result in disqualification. |
